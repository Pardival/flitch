# Code of Conduct

Flitch follows the **Contributor Covenant, version 2.1**:
https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/

In short: be welcoming, be respectful, assume good intent, and focus on what is best for the community.
Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not tolerated.

To report unacceptable behaviour, open a private
[security advisory](https://github.com/Pardival/flitch/security/advisories/new) with "Conduct" in the title,
or contact the maintainer through their GitHub profile. Reports are handled confidentially.
